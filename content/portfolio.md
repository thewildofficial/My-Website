<p class="eyebrow">Selected work</p>

The work collected here is guided by a simple interest: building systems that make complex information more legible and useful.

## Epistemic Steering Architecture

<p class="project-meta">Research · 2026 · Python</p>

An active research program investigating whether lightweight probes can identify uncertainty in language models and route difficult requests before unnecessary computation is spent. The project treats negative results and confounds as first-class evidence, not details to hide.

[Read the preprint](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404629800_Epistemic_Steering_Using_Hidden-State_Probes_to_Route_LLM_Behavior_and_Prevent_Hallucinations) · [View the research repository](https://github.com/thewildofficial/epistemic-steering)

## Epistemic Probing Replication

<p class="project-meta">Research · 2026 · Interpretability</p>

A reproducible investigation into representations associated with model confidence. The work focuses on calibration, held-out evaluation, and the difference between a promising signal and a robust result.

[View the replication repository](https://github.com/thewildofficial/epistemic-probing-replication)

## GeopolMonitor

<p class="project-meta">Software · Archive · Python</p>

A configurable monitor for turning fast-moving geopolitical reporting into a more navigable information stream. It explored aggregation, ranking, alerts, and information design.

[View the repository](https://github.com/thewildofficial/GeopolMonitor)
